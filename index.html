How Blip finds your ball

Four moves, about as long as a practice swing. Keep playing the ball you like. Your iPhone is the screen, and Blip Cam adds the flash and camera.

  1. 01

    Mark it

    Before you tee off, draw your line on the ball with the Blip Pen.

  2. 02

    Snap it

    Clip Blip Cam to the back of your iPhone and point it where the ball went.

  3. 03

    Find it

    Blip fires a flash your eyes can't see, waits a split second, then looks. Grass and sunlight stay dark; your mark shows up.

  4. 04

    Walk to the dot

    Follow the dot on your screen to your ball.

Questions golfers ask about Blip

How do I find a lost golf ball?

Most lost balls are a few steps from where they landed: under a leaf, in a shadow or in the rough. Walk to where you saw it land and search outward. With Blip, you mark your ball with the Blip Pen before the round. When you lose it, point your iPhone with Blip Cam at the area and walk to the dot on screen.

Does Blip work with my own golf balls?

Yes. Blip works with any ball you mark with the Blip Pen. The ball stays exactly as it is, with nothing added inside.

Does Blip work in daylight?

Blip is designed for daylight, and we are testing that now. The prototype camera already spots a ball in the open. Its flash and capture are synced to the millionth of a second. The Blip Pen ink and a marked ball under leaves in full daylight are still in testing. We will post the results either way.

Is a golf ball finder legal in tournaments?

No. Blip is for casual rounds (Rule 4.3a). Under Rule 4.3a of the Rules of Golf, using equipment like this during a competition round is not allowed.

How is Blip different from UV flashlights, RF golf balls and ball-finder glasses?

Blue-tint glasses and filter apps only help when the ball is already visible. UV flashlights only work at night. RF tracking balls need special balls, about £26 each, and only work at close range. Blip works with your own ball and your iPhone. It is designed for daylight and for balls under leaves, which we are testing now.
